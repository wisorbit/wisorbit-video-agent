Prepaid passes — no auto-renewal

All our paid plans (Starter, Pro, Creator, Pro yearly) and the minute pack are one-time prepaid payments. There is no subscription, no auto-debit and no automatic renewal. When a pass ends you move back to the Free plan automatically and nothing is charged.

Cancellation

Because there are no recurring charges, you don't need to do anything to cancel — no further payment will ever be taken automatically. You can cancel a video that is still waiting in line (not yet started) yourself; its minutes come back instantly.

When you get a full refund

  • Money was debited but the plan/minutes were not activated within 24 hours.
  • You were charged twice (duplicate payment) for the same order.
  • You bought a plan by mistake and have not used any of its minutes — email us within 7 days of purchase.

Minutes returned (automatic)

If a video cannot be made for a technical reason, or is cancelled, its minutes are returned to your account automatically.

When refunds are not given

  • For minutes already used, or for unused minutes after the pass period has ended.
  • For accounts closed for breaking the Terms & Conditions.

How to request a refund

Email pkdnalanda@gmail.com from your login email with the order/payment ID (on your Razorpay receipt) and the reason. We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI/card/bank) and usually reach you in 5–7 working days.
