This website and service (WisOrbit AI Video Agent, video.wisorbit.com) is operated by Preetam Kumar Deepak, Gaya, Bihar, India. Protecting your information matters to us. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (and the name/email Google shares if you sign in with Google).
  • Videos: the topics you type, the settings you choose (style, language, length) and the generated video files.
  • Plan and payments: your plan, minutes left, order/payment IDs, amount and date. We never store card, UPI or bank details — those go directly to Razorpay.

How we use it

Only to log you in, make and deliver your videos, track your plan and minutes, confirm payments, provide support and keep the service secure. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Service providers

  • Supabase — login, database and video storage.
  • Render — website hosting.
  • Razorpay — payments.
  • AI, voice and image services — receive your topic/script text to make the video (not your email).

How long we keep it

  • Video files are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Account and payment records are kept while your account exists, or as long as the law requires (for example tax and accounting).

Cookies

Your browser stores a login session (local storage) to keep you signed in, plus your language choice. We use no tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

To see or correct your information, or to have your account and data deleted, email pkdnalanda@gmail.com. We act within 7 working days.
